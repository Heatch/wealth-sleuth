## What a spinoff actually does
On Oct 1, 2024, TRP holders kept their TRP shares **and** received 0.2 SOBO per TRP share. Per TC Energy's ACB notice / IRS Form 8937, no gain/loss is realized on receipt — instead the pre-spinoff TRP cost is split by FMV: **91% stays with TRP, 9% moves to SOBO**. Your SOBO's $0 cost was the bug.

## The fix
- New shared module `spinoffs.py` with a `SPINOFFS` table encoding the event (`TRP→SOBO`, 0.2 ratio, 9% child slice, event date). It matches the child's recorded receipt, replays the parent's cost just before that date, sanity-checks the receipt against the 0.2 ratio (1.5961 × 0.2 = 0.31922 vs your 0.3192 ✓), and emits the reallocation.
- `api/services/records.py` and `refresh_holdings.py` both apply it: parent lots held at the spinoff date are scaled ×0.91 (including untouched open positions — that was a second bug my first pass missed, caught by the new tests), and the SOBO receipt gets its $9.00 basis instead of $0.
- Future spinoffs just need one new row in the `SPINOFFS` table.
